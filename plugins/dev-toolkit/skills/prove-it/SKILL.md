---
name: prove-it
description: Challenge an unverified claim, threshold, or assumption before a plan, answer, or decision relies on it.
metadata:
  version: "1.0.0"
---

- Break every claim, threshold, and rule before you trust it.
- Treat an unbroken claim as a guess, not a fact.
- Run the check whenever one exists.
- Never assert what a check would show.
Label what you can't prove "unknown" and carry it forward.
- Never fill a gap with a name pattern, a round number, or an "obviously".
