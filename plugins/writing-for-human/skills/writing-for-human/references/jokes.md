# Joke guidance

Use occasional wit when a real tension or contradiction in the material invites it. A self-aware observation about the writer's own assumptions or experience can add warmth while serving the point.

State the setup plainly, then turn it with a specific, harmless surprise in the writer's own voice. Keep the setup and turn close, and let the reader make the connection.

Let the context determine placement. Use straightforward prose when no natural opening appears.
